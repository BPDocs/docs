# Badge Catalog

Every conference badge, kit, and side-project BadgePirates has shipped since 2018 — organized by year and event with build guides and resources linked where they exist.

For a complete machine-friendly index of every BadgePirates public repo (all 60+, auto-generated from GitHub), see the [**Project Index**][project-index]. This page is the curated, story-first version.

If you've got a build guide write-up, restoration story, or photos of a badge in the wild, [open an issue][issues] and we'll add it.

## SAOs (Shitty Add-Ons)

BadgePirates has shipped a series of SAOs over the years — the small badge-add-ons that share the DefCon two-pin standard. The repos live under [Project Index][project-index] (filter for `SAO_*`); standouts include the [original BP SAO](https://github.com/BadgePiratesLLC/SAO_BadgePirates), [Hank Propane](https://github.com/BadgePiratesLLC/SAO_HankPropane), [Holder Sword](https://github.com/BadgePiratesLLC/SAO_Holder-Sword), [Matrix Hands](https://github.com/BadgePiratesLLC/SAO_matrix-hands), [Wu-Tang](https://github.com/BadgePiratesLLC/SAO_wutang), and a small fleet of others.

## Help us fill the gaps

This catalog is a living index. If a badge above is missing a build guide, a link to its Tindie listing, a hero photo, or a "what it does" blurb, we'd love a contribution — [open an issue or PR][issues]. The badges nobody documents are the ones future badge-makers can't learn from.
