# How can I buy a Badge Pirates badge?

There are two main ways to get a BadgePirates badge: buy from our online store, or grab one in person at a conference where we have a table.

## Online — Tindie

Our [Tindie store](https://www.tindie.com/stores/badgepirates/) is the easiest way to buy from us when there's no event nearby. You'll find:

- Past conference badges (when stock allows)
- Simple Add-Ons (SAOs) — small companion boards that plug into other badges
- Learn-to-Solder kits — beginner-friendly assembly kits
- The occasional one-off project or experimental design

Stock varies. If something you want is sold out, hop on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/BfsYbHY8m7) and ask — we may have a few set aside or know of an upcoming run.

## In person — at a conference

We bring badges to the events we attend. If you're at a con and we're there, look for our table in the **Solder Village** (or the equivalent maker / hardware area). You can usually buy current and recent badges directly, no shipping required.

If we're attending an event with a dedicated conference badge — like BSidesKC — that badge is often available **only at the event** during the conference itself, with leftover stock going to Tindie afterward.

## Newsletter

Want a heads-up when new badges drop or when we're heading to your city? [Sign up for the BadgePirates newsletter](https://badgepirates.com/) (the form is on the homepage). One email per month, no spam.
