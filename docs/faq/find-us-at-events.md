# How can I find BadgePirates at an event?

When we attend a conference, we set up in the **Solder Village** — the area of the con dedicated to hardware tinkering, soldering kits, and live electronics demos. If a con doesn't have a Solder Village, we'll be in whatever the nearest equivalent is (Hardware Hacking Village, Maker space, etc.).

## What we usually have at the table

- **Current and recent badges** for sale
- **Learn-to-Solder kits** in multiple difficulty levels — we'll often help you build one on the spot
- **SAOs** to add to your badge collection
- **A reflash station** — bring an old BadgePirates badge and we'll update it with the latest firmware
- **Demos and works-in-progress** — we like showing off the latest experiments

## Solder kits at events

Many of our recent conference badges have been **build-it-yourself solder kits** with two difficulty modes:

- **Easy mode** — through-hole and surface-mount mix, approachable for beginners
- **Hard mode** — all surface-mount, for the steady-handed and brave

If you've never soldered before, don't worry — we'll walk you through it. The Solder Village is the friendliest place at any conference.

## Where we've been

We've attended events including BSidesKC, CactusCon, DEF CON SecKC events, BSides STL, BSides CHS, BioHacking Village (DC30/DC31), OzSec, ToorCamp, SANSHackfest, and SecKC meetups. The full archive of badges by event is on our [GitHub](https://github.com/BadgePiratesLLC).
